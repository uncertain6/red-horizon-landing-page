The journeys

Three ways to experience
the red planet

Every voyage is different. Whether you want to witness Mars from orbit or call it home for a year — there's a mission built for you.

Orbiter

The flyby

A 10-day orbital voyage. Watch dust storms swirl across Olympus Mons from a panoramic viewport — no landing, pure spectacle.

Pioneer

The expedition

A 30-day mission with a full week on the surface. Guided rover treks across Valles Marineris and nights under two moons.

Colonist

The residency

A full Martian year at Horizon Base One. Live, work, and help build humanity's first permanent home beyond Earth.
